Bazaar — free Bootstrap 5 marketplace template by uiCookies
===========================================================

Concept
-------
"Bazaar" is the busy everything-store: a dense, utilitarian multi-category
marketplace for electronics, home, audio and gaming — the opposite of a calm
single-brand boutique. The idea was to make it feel like a real superstore you
scan rather than read, driven by information density and functional colour
coding: one brand colour, one deal colour, one urgency colour, each doing a
single job. It ships two pages — a department-packed storefront (index.html)
with a vanilla deal carousel, a live flash-sale countdown and a filterable
12-product best-seller grid, and a genuine product page (product.html) with an
image gallery, colour/edition variants, a quantity stepper and review tabs — all
in dependency-free vanilla JavaScript. No jQuery, no icon fonts, no CDN.

Palette
-------
Teal        #0d5c63  brand: header marks, nav, primary buttons, links
Teal deep   #073f45  hover / hero gradient
Marigold    #f6a609  DEALS: search button, deal CTAs, cart badge, star ratings
Signal red  #e23744  URGENCY only: flash sale, discount %, low-stock, wishlist-on
Ink         #17202e  blue-tinted near-black: top bar, footer, headings
Paper       #f4f6fa  cool blue-grey page canvas (not warm cream)
Line        #e2e7ef  hairline borders / tile fills

Cool blue-grey paper + deep teal deliberately reads "trustworthy tech
superstore" and stays clearly distinct from the warm bone + cobalt + flame of
the Shopfront boutique in this same category.

Type pairing
------------
Display : Big Shoulders Display (700 / 800) — condensed industrial signage face,
          used for the logo, hero banners and section titles like market signage
Body    : IBM Plex Sans (400-600) — utilitarian, tech-legible, great for dense
          product copy, prices and specs
Labels  : IBM Plex Mono (500 / 600) — eyebrows, prices, SKUs, countdown & stock
          counters, for a spec-sheet retail feel

Fonts load from Google Fonts. Bootstrap 5 is self-hosted in css/vendor and
js/vendor and referenced by relative path. All icons are hand-written inline
SVG. Footer carries "(c) 2026" and "Free template by uiCookies".
